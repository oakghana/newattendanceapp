### Features

#### Dismissing the Toast
- Click the **X** button in the top-right corner
- Toast remembers dismissal for that day
- Returns with updated count the next day
- Helps keep your dashboard clean

#### Automatic Updates
- Countdown updates every minute
- No need to refresh
- Shows real-time remaining days

#### Information Provided
- **Days Remaining**: Large, easy-to-read countdown
- **Resume Date**: When you should return to work
- **Leave Type**: Your leave category (Casual, Annual, Sick, etc.)
- **Personalized Message**: Based on your leave status

### Panel Information

Each staff member card shows:
- **Name**: Full name of the staff on leave
- **Department**: Their department code/name
- **Leave Duration**: Start date – End date (e.g., 27 Jul – 03 Aug)
- **Leave Type**: Category of leave (Casual, Annual, Sick, etc.)
- **Days Remaining**: Color-coded urgency badge

### Color-Coded Urgency Badges

| Color | Meaning | Status | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|--------|
| Green | Low urgency | 5+ days remaining | Monitor |
| Blue | Medium urgency | 2-5 days remaining | Prepare workspace |
| Amber | High urgency | 1-2 days remaining | Notify team |
| Red | Critical urgency | Today or Overdue | Urgent action |

### What It Shows
- **Only currently on leave**: Staff with active leaves only
- **Department visibility**: HOD sees their department only
- **Region visibility**: RM sees all departments in their region
- **Full visibility**: HR and Admin see all company staff

### How It Helps

1. **Workforce Planning**: Know who's available each day
2. **Workload Distribution**: Reassign work before they return
3. **Coverage Planning**: Plan shifts and responsibilities
4. **Leave Tracking**: Monitor leave patterns and anomalies
5. **Meeting Scheduling**: Avoid scheduling meetings with absent staff
6. **Project Management**: Adjust timelines based on availability

### Automatic Updates
- Panel refreshes every 5 minutes
- Always shows current data
- No manual refresh needed
- Real-time tracking

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Staff on 3-Day Casual Leave
**What Staff Sees**:
- Day 1: Green toast, "3 days remaining"
- Day 2: Green toast, "2 days remaining"
- Day 3: Amber toast, "1 day remaining"
- Day 4: Red toast, "Resume work today!"

**What HOD Sees**:
- Staff appears in monitoring panel with "3 days left"
- After each day, count decreases
- When back to work, staff disappears from panel

### Scenario 2: Staff Leave Ends Today
**What Staff Sees**:
- Red toast with "🎉 Resume Work Today!"
- "0 days remaining" in countdown

**What HOD Sees**:
- Staff card shows "ENDS TODAY" badge in red
- Urgent reminder to prepare workspace

### Scenario 3: Overdue Staff (Didn't Check In)
**What Staff Sees**:
- Red toast "🔴 Time to Check In"
- Message: "Your leave has ended. Please check in"

**What HOD Sees**:
- Staff card shows "Overdue" status
- Can follow up with staff

## Tips for Best Use

### For Staff
1. **Don't ignore the toast** - It's your countdown to work
2. **Prepare in advance** - When toast turns amber, start preparing
3. **Plan return** - Set reminders for items to complete before return
4. **Check communications** - While on leave, check important messages

### For HOD/RM
1. **Review daily** - Check the panel each morning
2. **Plan coverage** - Adjust schedules before staff return
3. **Prepare workspace** - Ensure desk/area is ready
4. **Update team** - Inform team about upcoming returns
5. **Follow up on overdue** - Contact staff if they don't check in

## Troubleshooting

### Toast Not Appearing?
- Check if you're actually on approved leave
- Verify leave dates are set correctly in the system
- Try logging out and back in

### Wrong Days Showing?
- Toast updates every minute automatically
- If still wrong, refresh the page
- Check that leave end date is correct in your profile

### Monitoring Panel Empty?
- You may not have the required role (HOD/RM/HR/Admin)
- There may be no staff currently on leave
- Try refreshing the page
